ℹ NOTES — which columns to weigh when buying manually
Tier 1 — gate conditions (must pass first)
  • BUY INTEL — composite penalty score. FALLING KNIFE or CHASING (3-4) tags mean the bot's own gates are already penalizing it — avoid regardless of other numbers.
  • CONV — core conviction score. Negative CONV is a bot no-buy by the live LB_MIN_SCORE gate; don't override it manually without a specific reason.
Tier 2 — quality confirmation
  • WHALE — smart-money accumulation. >60/100 is meaningfully strong; <30-40 is thin support.
  • BULLCONF — confluence checks passing. ≥6/10 is solid; 4-5/10 is mediocre.
  • 4H BIAS / DAILY BIAS — want these aligned bullish. Conflicting timeframes (e.g. 4H bull, Daily bear) plus a knife/chase tag is a classic trap.
Tier 3 — momentum, secondary confirmation
  • CVD / OI MOM — want up/ACCELERATING, not FADING. FADING means momentum is weakening even if price looks fine.
  • RSI15 — avoid buying into extension (roughly >65-70); low-to-mid 50s-60s has more room to run.
Overrides everything above
  • Check the regime banner above (Regime / Breadth / Risk score). A RISK_OFF regime with breadth/risk failing the bot's own BUY_MIN_BREADTH / BUY_BTC_RISK_MAX gates means even a clean-looking row is fighting a market-wide headwind the bot itself would likely block or discount right now.
